How to Stop Google Chat website from taking over Alt-B or Control-B
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem:  WHen I connect to Google Chat, the Alt-B and Control-B shortcuts do not work
Solution: Set permissions.default.shortcuts = 2


Procedure
---------
 1. Start Firefox
 2. In the URL, type-in about:config
 3. Search for:  permissions.default.shortcuts
 4. Set the value to 2
 5. Restart Firefox
	-- Now, Alt-B will open the bookmarks menu in Google Chat
